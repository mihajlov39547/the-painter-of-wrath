\parbox{0.76\textwidth}{
\textbf{The Painter of Wrath} is a rewritten, reframed, and expanded
non-commercial adaptation of \emph{Deus Irae} by Philip K. Dick
and Roger Zelazny.

\bigskip

This edition preserves the moral and philosophical core of the
original while reshaping its structure, voice, and thematic
emphasis for contemporary readers. It adds a prologue,
multiple epilogues, and archival materials that explore legacy,
belief, restraint, and the ethics of truth.
