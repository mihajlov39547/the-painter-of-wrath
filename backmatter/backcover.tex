\parbox{0.76\textwidth}{
\textbf{The Painter of Wrath} is a non-commercial, academic and
critical reinterpretation of \emph{Deus Irae} by Philip K. Dick
and Roger Zelazny.

\bigskip

This edition reimagines the original novel’s structure and voice
while preserving its moral and philosophical core. It introduces
a new prologue, multiple epilogues, and archival-style materials
to examine belief, authority, kindness, and restraint in a
post-catastrophic world.

\bigskip

Prepared for study and critical engagement, this work is offered
as a tribute to the original text and as a means of bringing its
ideas into conversation with contemporary readers.
}
